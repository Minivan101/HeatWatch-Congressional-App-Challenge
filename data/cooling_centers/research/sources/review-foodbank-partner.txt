TEFAP Distributions 
 
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA: 
 
• Nevada residency may be established by the recipient self-declaring they are a resident of Nevada.  
• A household may be eligible for TEFAP USDA foods by self-declaration that the total household income is at 
or below the current TEFAP Income Eligibility, which is 185% of the current Federal poverty income guideline.  
• OR Participation in any one of the following programs also qualiﬁes a household to receive TEFAP Foods 
(Household is NOT required to show documentation or proof of participation) 
o Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) 
o Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) 
o Supplemental Security Income (SSI) 
o Medicaid 
o Homeless people are presumed to be residents of the State and therefore are eligible to receive a 
meal in a Soup kitchen 
o Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservation (FDPIR) 
o Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP)
